04 · Your earnings

10% of every sale, paid every quarter

Metamorphosis receives 10% of gross sales, paid by Interac e-Transfer on full months. Nothing is deducted for products, refills, card fees, repairs or our time.

05 · The work behind it

What it takes to keep the machine full

A vending machine looks simple from the outside. Behind it there is buying, driving, restocking, cleaning, handling cash, fixing jams and failed card payments, and a daily review of the sales. We do all of it, and we use AI to plan every refill from the machine's own data.

Estimated from our visit records: twice a week from March to June, about weekly since July. Each visit counts 7.5 hours of Buenavyda time.

The value Buenavyda adds to Metamorphosis

Everything below comes to Metamorphosis at no cost: the equipment, the products, the work and the travel. Your team and clients get a full-service vending machine, and Metamorphosis earns a commission on top.

Equipment values come from the supplier invoices. Service hours and travel are valued at what the same work would cost Metamorphosis if it hired someone to do it. Restocking after the first load is paid from sales, so it is not counted twice.

06 · Why keep Buenavyda operating

Owning a machine vs. having us run it

If Metamorphosis bought its own machines

  • Pays for the machine, card reader and monthly payment-system fees up front.
  • Buys and stores the stock, and absorbs expired or damaged products.
  • Assigns staff to refill, clean, count cash and deal with jams and refunds.
  • Keeps the full sales, but also every cost and every hour.

With Buenavyda (today)

  • $0 invested, no staff time, no stock risk.
  • 10% of every sale, paid quarterly, with this live report to check it.
  • Products chosen from real sales data and from your team's requests.
  • AI-planned refills: each visit brings exactly what is missing.
  • We add new services at your clinics only when they make sense for you.

07 · The next step

An essentials corner, paid from the phone

Your team told us that clients and staff often leave to buy basics at the nearby store. Buenavyda GrabGo turns a small shelf into a self-service corner: toothbrushes, toothpaste, soap, paper products and other essentials. People take what they need, send a photo by text message, and pay with their card or Apple Pay / Google Pay. Like the vending machine, we stock it, run it and pay Metamorphosis a commission, with no investment from you.

See it at your clinic
